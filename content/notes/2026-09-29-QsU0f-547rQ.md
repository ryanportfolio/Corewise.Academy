## Lesson material

- Prerequisite: know what a skill packages; link *Expertise you can install*.

- Exercise: preserve a failed request, draft a narrow manual skill, rerun it and another known case, then compare one candidate deletion.
- Short quote candidate: Austin Marchese at 10:58, "If you don't have to send back the result, the skill is working." Caption wording confirmed; use only with a caveat about checking correctness.
